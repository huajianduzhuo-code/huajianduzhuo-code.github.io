DMAST combines imitation learning, oracle-guided supervised fine-tuning, and adversarial reinforcement learning with GRPO self-play to jointly train a web agent and an attacker. The framework improves robustness to cross-modal attacks and task completion on unseen tasks.

**Authors**: Haoyu Liu, Dingcheng Li, Lukas Rutishauser, Zeyu Zheng

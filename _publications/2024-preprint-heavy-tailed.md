This paper analyzes the limitations of existing diffusion models when learning and generating heavy-tailed data. It develops a method for simulating multivariate heavy-tailed distributions and conditional distributions with multidimensional conditions.

**Authors**: H. Liu†, T. Zhu†, N. Jia, J. He, Z. Zheng (†co-first author)
